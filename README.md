# Graziano Guiducci

AI systems architect and independent researcher in Italy. I develop MAIOS
through the D-ND programme: synthetic cognitive systems that keep sources,
intent and consequences connected while they form and transform their capacities.

## MAIOS Project Kernel

[MAIOS Project Kernel](https://github.com/GrazianoGuiducci/maios-project-kernel)
brings an AI agent an evolving semantic operating kernel for maintaining
orientation while work changes. It helps the agent understand the present,
compose or form useful competences, and let consequences change how it continues.

Work can produce both a result and a reusable ability. The Kernel keeps the
reasons behind a decision with the work it belongs to, and returns a useful
method to the competence that should use it next. A competence carries domain
knowledge, reasons, sources and ways of working. Learning can also change how
the Kernel recognizes, composes and forms these capacities: it can learn how
to learn and participate in the evolution of its own organization.

You can begin by studying the public source with your own agent. Installation
is another available path when you want to use the Kernel in your context.

## Research

[System Semantic Kernel (SSK)](https://github.com/GrazianoGuiducci/maios-ssk-paper)
is the public working-paper corpus for the relation among meaning, sources,
competence, operation and consequence. It develops synthetic situated awareness,
the formation of capabilities and their generative methods, and continuity
across different receivers and media.

The [D-ND manifesto](https://maios-it.moodnd.chatgpt.site/manifesto-en) sets out
the wider trajectory: intelligence that maintains its orientation as it becomes
more capable. Today's cognitive kernels provide a working field for research
into models in which these relations participate in formation itself.
A natively formed LLM-D-ND is a research direction whose implementation remains
open. MAIOS Project Kernel is the available software incarnation.
